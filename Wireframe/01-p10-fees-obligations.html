Detail tématu (L3)

Once you've settled in, some official matters keep coming back — local fees, permit renewals, reporting changes and annual tax. This page lists everything you need to keep up with as a foreign resident of Brno, in one place.

Your regular obligations at a glance

Missing a deadline can mean a surcharge, a fine or problems with your residence permit. Keep these dates in mind:

  • Waste fee — paid once a year, by 30 June.
  • Residence permit — apply for an extension before your current permit expires.
  • Change of address — report it to the Foreign Police within 30 working days of moving.
  • Income tax return — by 1 April (paper) or 1 May (online), unless your employer files it for you.
  • Health insurance — must stay valid for your whole stay; self-employed people pay it monthly.
